Minjie Jia

Degree Ph.D., Statistics and Data Science
Email Address mj598@cornell.edu

Minjie Jia is a Ph.D. student in the Department of Statistics and Data Science at Cornell University. She earned her B.S. in Quantitative Finance from the University of Hong Kong. Her research interests center on time series forecasting and uncertainty quantification in machine learning.

Currently, Minjie’s work focuses on developing reliable prediction intervals for dependent data and leveraging statistical learning methods in interdisciplinary applications across economics and public policy.

Outside of her academic pursuits, Minjie enjoys photography and snowboarding.
