Anna is a sophomore in the College of Agriculture and Life Sciences, majoring in Global Development. She has worked in environmental policy as both a lobbyist and a congressional assistant, coordinating collaborations for clean energy firms, supporting policy communication, and developing an understanding of domestic agricultural networks. Her appreciation for agriculture stems from extensive work with her hometown farmers’ market networks, and she has recently begun partnering with the Kenyan Nyangala Resource Centre to study local agribusiness systems and management.
